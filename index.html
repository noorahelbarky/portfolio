Venture research

I’m developing a consumer apparel venture and keeping the concept itself under wraps while I build. What I can share is the process: I’m testing the idea with real customers before I invest in it.

Problem. Before building anything, I wanted evidence that shoppers have an unmet need in this space, in their own words.

What I did. Designed and ran a primary-research survey that has gathered 70+ responses since the start of the semester and is still open.

9%of respondents rated 9 or 10 out of 10 on how often they find exactly what they’re looking for
82%of respondents said they’d love to buy from a brand in this space that welcomes all backgrounds
Top themeRespondents named marketing, standing out, and finding first customers as the hardest parts of launching a brand

Open-ended answers also kept returning to fit and sizing consistency, and to quality at a fair price.

What changed. The survey changed my target audience. I found who would actually want to buy my products, which was different from my original vision.

I’m still taking participants, so if you’d like to weigh in, the survey is open below.
